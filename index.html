External +5VAll six red wires
External GNDAll six brown wires + Arduino GND
USBArduino Uno only
Power: use a regulated 5V 5A supply for the servos. Never feed six servos from the Uno 5V pin or USB. Do not connect external +5V to the Uno 5V pin while USB is connected. Connect grounds together.

Build & test

  1. Assemble base, shoulder, upper arm, forearm, wrist and gripper.
  2. With horns disconnected, upload the sketch; it moves all joints to the neutral home angles.
  3. Power off and fit horns with the joints near their neutral positions.
  4. Connect servo red/brown wires to external 5V/GND distribution; signals to D2–D7.
  5. Join external GND to Arduino GND. Power Uno via USB and servos from the external supply.
  6. Test each servo without load. Stop if it buzzes, stalls, heats or binds.
  7. Adjust limits and horn offsets in code for your physical mechanism.

Optional: 1000µF, 10V (or higher) electrolytic capacitor across servo +5V/GND near the distribution point; observe polarity.

Arduino Uno sketch

Use the built-in Arduino Servo library. Serial Monitor: 115200 baud, send newline-terminated commands such as A1:90, A2:110 … A6:55, or HOME. This browser simulation does not directly connect to the physical USB serial port.
